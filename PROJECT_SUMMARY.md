## 🎨 Design System Implementation

### Color Palette
- **Primary Blue**: #1e88e5 (actions, active states)
- **Secondary Background**: #f0f2f5 (page backgrounds)
- **Card Background**: #ffffff (content containers)
- **Text Primary**: #050505 (21:1 contrast)
- **Text Secondary**: #65676b (8.5:1 contrast)
- **Text Tertiary**: #90949c (6.5:1 contrast)
- **Border Color**: #e4e6eb (separators)
- **Status Green**: #27ae60 (success/approved)
- **Status Yellow**: #f39c12 (warnings)
- **Status Red**: #dc2626 (errors/delete)

### Typography
- **Font**: Segoe UI, Helvetica Neue, Arial
- **Scales**: Display (24px), H1 (20px), H2 (16px), Body (13-14px), Caption (11-12px)
- **Weights**: Regular (400), Bold (700)
- **Line Heights**: 1.4-1.5 for optimal readability

### Spacing
- **Base Unit**: 4px
- **Common Values**: 4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 20px, 24px, 32px
- **Card Padding**: 15-20px
- **Component Spacing**: 15-20px

### Visual Effects
- **Cards**: 12px border-radius with gaussian shadow
- **Buttons**: 8px border-radius with hover effects
- **Shadows**: (rgba(0,0,0,0.06), 6px blur)
- **Focus States**: 2px blue borders on inputs
